### Added

- git-master commit attribution can be turned off with `git config omc.attribution false` (or `sisyphus.attribution`) or with `NO_AI_ATTRIBUTION=1`, `OMC_NO_COMMIT_ATTRIBUTION=1` or `OMC_GIT_ATTRIBUTION=0`, which drops both the footer and the co-author trailer. `omc.attribution true` or `OMC_GIT_ATTRIBUTION=1` turns the builtin footer on and leaves `include_co_authored_by` as configured. ([#7833](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/oh-my-cybersecurity/pull/7833))
